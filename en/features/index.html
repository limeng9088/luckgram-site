More accounts in one app

Today Luckgram follows Telegram's standard limit for accounts on one device. We are making room for many more, so personal, work and community accounts can sit side by side and you switch between them from Settings.

Built on Telegram, open to everyone

The Telegram network

Luckgram signs in to your Telegram account and follows Telegram's updates, so chats, channels, calls and bots work as you expect.

Your messages stay with Telegram

Messages are sent to and stored on Telegram's servers. Luckgram runs no message servers of its own.

Open source

The code for iOS and Android is public, and every release is built from it by GitHub Actions.
