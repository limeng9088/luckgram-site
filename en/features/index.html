In development

More accounts in one app

Today Luckgram follows Telegram's standard limit of 3 accounts on one device. We are removing the cap on both iOS and Android, so personal, work, client and channel-admin accounts sit side by side, each with its own notifications, and you switch between them in a tap.

Read more in the Help Center

In development

Back up your accounts and move them to a new phone

Choose the accounts you want and save them to one backup file locked with your own password. Restore it on a new phone or after reinstalling, without signing in to each account again, and move between iPhone and Android with the same file.

Read more in the Help Center

In development

Double Bottom: a second passcode with its own view

Set a second passcode that opens Luckgram with only the accounts you pick. Your usual passcode still opens everything, so you decide what shows up when someone else is looking.

Read more in the Help Center

In development

Hide chats without deleting them

Swipe a chat out of your list without deleting it or leaving the group. Hidden chats stay one gesture away whenever you need them.

Read more in the Help Center

Available (Telegram feature)

Read messages in your language

Tap Translate and messages in another language switch to yours, right in the chat. This is Telegram's own translation: single messages are free, and translating a whole chat at once needs Telegram Premium.

Read more in the Help Center

In development

Translate what you write before you send it

Write in the language you think in, then translate the text in the input bar and send it in your contact's language. You see the result before anything is sent.

Read more in the Help Center

Telegram Premium; free version in development

Read voice messages, dictate your replies

Turn voice messages and round videos of any length into text with one tap, handy in a meeting or on a noisy train. Dictate instead of typing and watch the words appear as you speak; optional AI cleanup removes filler words and fixes punctuation. Telegram offers transcription with Premium; a free version for Luckgram is being built.

Read more in the Help Center

Answer with saved quick replies

In development

Record calls, with a transcript and AI summary

Record a call with one tap, on iPhone and Android. When you hang up, the recording lands in Saved Messages or any chat you choose, with a searchable transcript and an AI summary of what was agreed. Recording laws differ by country; let the other person know before you record.

Read more in the Help Center

Available; extra controls in development

See what takes up space and clear it

Storage Usage shows how much space videos, photos, files and music take on your phone, and you clear the types you choose. Coming next: pick any cache size from 1 GB to no limit, keep media from one hour to forever, and set different rules for each chat.

Keyword folders that find chats for you
