Available

10 accounts free, unlimited with Pro

Telegram allows 3 accounts on one device. Luckgram lets you sign in to 10 for free, and as many as you like with Luckgram Pro, so personal, work, client and channel-admin accounts sit side by side, each with its own notifications, and you switch between them in a tap.

Read more in the Help Center

Available

Double Bottom: a second passcode with its own view

Set a second passcode that opens Luckgram with only the accounts you pick. Your usual passcode still opens everything, so you decide what shows up when someone else is looking.

Read more in the Help Center

Available

Hide chats without deleting them

Swipe a chat out of your list without deleting it or leaving the group. Hidden chats stay one gesture away whenever you need them.

Read more in the Help Center

Available

Open channels the store apps hide, for free

Some channels and groups are hidden only in the App Store and Google Play builds of Telegram. Turn on one switch in Settings and Luckgram opens them, free and permanently, with no website login. Channels that Telegram blocks for everyone, or that are blocked where you live, stay blocked.

Read more in the Help Center

Available

Read messages in your language

Press and hold a message and tap Translate: the translation appears right under the original, in the same bubble, and Undo Translate puts it back. Or turn on the one-tap translate button next to messages in another language. The first time, you pick the language to translate into, and Luckgram remembers it; tap the language line on any translation to change it. Voice messages can be translated once they are turned into text. Free, with no Telegram Premium needed: when Telegram can't translate, Luckgram's own translation takes over.

Read more in the Help Center

Available

Translate what you write before you send it

Write in the language you think in, then tap Translate in the + panel, or hold the send button and choose Translate or Translate to…. The text is replaced in the input bar, so you see it before anything is sent, and the language is remembered for each chat.

Read more in the Help Center

Available

Read voice messages, dictate your replies

Turn voice messages and round videos of any length into text with one tap, handy in a meeting or on a noisy train. Dictate instead of typing and watch the words appear as you speak; optional AI cleanup removes filler words and fixes punctuation. Free, no Telegram Premium needed.

Read more in the Help Center

A cleaner input bar with everything under +

The input bar keeps just emoji, the text field, voice and a + button. Tap + for a grid with Photos, Camera, File, Location, Contact and Poll, plus Quick replies, Voice input, AI writing and Translate. Prefer the old layout? Turn the compact input bar off in Luckgram Features.

Pro

Luckgram Pro: more AI, more accounts, no sponsored messages

Luckgram Pro is Luckgram's own membership, separate from Telegram Premium. It raises your daily AI and translation limits, adds monthly AI credits for the premium model, longer chat summaries with follow-up questions, Pro-only agents, unlimited accounts and cloud backup, and hides Telegram's sponsored messages in channels. The subscription page shows the current perks and your remaining credits.

Read more in the Help Center

Pro

Back up your accounts to the cloud, encrypted

Pick the accounts to back up and set a backup password. The backup is encrypted on your phone before upload, so Luckgram stores only data it cannot read. On a new phone, iPhone or Android, sign in to any one account, choose Restore from cloud, enter the password and your accounts come back without signing in to each one. Forget the password and the backup cannot be restored.

Available

Add proxies from a QR code, a link or a subscription, even before you sign in

Can't reach Telegram? The proxy button sits in the top-right corner of every sign-in screen, so you can add a SOCKS5 or MTProto proxy before you get your code. There are now two faster ways to add one. Scan a proxy QR code or paste a tg://, t.me or socks5:// link, and it goes straight into your list, with no server and port to type. Or paste a Clash or mihomo subscription URL to import all its SOCKS5 proxies at once; refresh it with one tap, and deleting the subscription removes the proxies it added. The proxy list shows the exit country and IP of every SOCKS5 proxy, and a Direct row at the top shows your own exit without one. After signing in, find Proxy settings at the top of the ⋮ menu in the chat list.

Read more in the Help Center
