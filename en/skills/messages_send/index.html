Send messages

Send a text message, to one chat at a time.

Sends one plain text message from one account to one chat, through the app's normal sending. There is no bulk tool and none is planned. You confirm once when you switch it on, and every message counts against the limit.

What it can do

  • Send a text message of up to 4096 characters
  • Reply to a specific message
  • Send from any logged-in account without switching to it

Ask your AI tool

  • Draft a reply to Li's last message, show it to me, and send it when I say so.
  • Tell the “Project A” group from my work account that the meeting moved to 3 pm.

Switch it on

  1. In Luckgram Desktop open Settings → Luckgram → Local MCP server.
  2. Under Skills of each account, find the account.
  3. Switch on Send messages. Luckgram asks you to confirm, once for this account.

From the command line

luckgram messages send 562953914961921 "The meeting moved to 3 pm" --account 7943234085
luckgram messages send @some_user --stdin --reply-to 4812 --account 7943234085 < reply.txt

With the luckgram skill for agents.

Good to know

  • queued means the app accepted the message. It is delivered, or shown as failed in the chat, like a message typed by hand.
  • Unsolicited messages to people who did not ask for them get accounts restricted by Telegram. The limit is there for that reason and cannot be switched off.
